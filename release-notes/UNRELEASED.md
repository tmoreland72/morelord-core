# Unreleased

## What Changed

- Sidebar multi-select checkboxes are the leftmost control on every directory row and use Foundry's flat checkbox, with a visible square and a dark checkmark on the accent fill. Actor, item, roll table, card, and macro checkboxes sit to the left of the thumbnail. Playlist checkboxes are the first control in the playlist header. Scene checkboxes stay on the upper left of the scene card and no longer activate the scene when another module listens for row clicks. Shift-click selects the visible range.
