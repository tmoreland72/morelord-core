Your character has a story beyond the open sheet.

Bring your Foundry characters to My Characters on the Morelord Gaming website. Morelord Character Export lets GMs and players export D&D characters directly from their character sheets and import them into My Characters after registering for a free website account.

  • Export from the sheet. Open any character sheet and choose Morelord Export from its title-bar controls.
  • Import into My Characters. Register for a free account on the Morelord Gaming website, open My Characters, and import your downloaded character JSON file.
  • Carry the character's underlying detail. Include source Actor data, embedded Items, item activities, and Active Effects.
  • Preserve the prepared snapshot. Include display-ready values already calculated by Foundry and D&D 5e.
  • Bring available artwork. Embed character and Item images in a library that avoids storing the same asset repeatedly.
  • Keep the source context. Record the Foundry and system versions with the export.

Exports are character snapshots, not live synchronization. Exporting from Foundry does not require Morelord Core or an account connection; importing into My Characters requires a free Morelord Gaming website account.

Requires Foundry VTT v14 and a supported D&D 5e ApplicationV2 character sheet. The manifest records D&D 5e 5.3.3 as verified; legacy ApplicationV1 sheets are not supported.
