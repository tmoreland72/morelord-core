Make every shopping trip part of the adventure.

The party has gold to spend, gear to sell, and a world to explore. Give them a marketplace worth opening. Morelord Marketplace turns your enabled D&D 5e item sources into a searchable catalog where players can find what they need and handle purchases and sales inside Foundry VTT.

  • Find the right gear. Search and filter by item type, rarity, source, price, and more instead of digging through compendiums.
  • Keep shopping moving. Build Buy and Sell carts with multiple items and quantities, then complete the transaction together.
  • Shop as a party. Scene shops let you choose who receives the items and which character or Group actor pays.
  • Give your world its own merchants. Premium Shop Manager adds configurable vendors with stock, pricing, reputation modifiers, restocking, and buy/sell rules.
  • Keep the GM in control. Premium transaction approvals let the GM review player purchases and sales before they commit.

The Standard global Marketplace covers browsing, buying, and selling. Add Premium features when your campaign calls for managed transactions and distinctive scene shops. Your existing world data stays in place if Premium access expires.

Requires Foundry VTT v14, a compatible D&D 5e system, and Morelord Core. Item availability comes from your enabled sources.
