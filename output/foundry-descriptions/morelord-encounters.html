The party went off the map. Have the next encounter ready.

Turn an unexpected turn in the story into a roster you can use. Morelord Encounters brings encounter preparation into Foundry VTT, helping you move from party levels and a difficulty target to linked monster Actors you can inspect and drag into the scene.

  • Compare six different approaches. Generate Pack Skirmish, Boss Battle, Boss and Minions, The Horde, Elite Team / Mirror Team, and Random alternatives.
  • Build exactly the roster you want. Custom mode combines a searchable monster browser with a live difficulty rating as you add creatures and change quantities.
  • Use your campaign's sources. Choose the permitted source books and keep the creature selection appropriate to your world.
  • Move from review to play. Open the linked Actor sheets, review the roster, and drag its Actor links into the scene.
  • Bring published encounters to the table. Use supported Drakkenheim encounters when the required source content is available.

Standard access supports available SRD creatures. Premium opens source selection to installed core and third-party monster books; those books are separate content. Difficulty budgets support your judgment while you account for terrain, tactics, and the party's strengths.

Requires Foundry VTT v14, D&D 5e 5.3 or later, and Morelord Core.
