Turn hard-won materials into the party's next ambition.

The creature is defeated. The rare ingredient is found. What will the party make of it? Morelord Craftworks connects gathering, harvesting, loot, materials, recipes, and crafting in Foundry VTT, giving the things characters recover a purpose beyond another line in their inventory.

  • Make discovery rewarding. Run gathering, monster harvesting, encounter loot, and treasure hoards with character inventory rewards.
  • Show players what is possible. Search materials and recipes, review ingredients and tools, and check the selected inventory against recipe requirements.
  • Support the way your party works. Use a shared Group inventory for materials while a separate character provides crafting skills and tools.
  • Finish what you start. Premium crafting execution tracks work and checks, consumes materials as appropriate, and awards completed outputs.
  • Prepare more of the rewards players love. Premium Potion, Spell Scroll, and Spellbook generators add specialized GM tools.

Standard features support acquisition, reference, and planning. Premium adds crafting execution and specialized utilities. Configurable Content Packs let you choose the material and recipe content that belongs in your campaign; some packs also require separately installed source products.

Requires Foundry VTT v14, D&D 5e 5.3 or later, Morelord Core, and socketlib.
