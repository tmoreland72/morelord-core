Keep the building blocks of your campaign close at hand.

A campaign grows from the people, places, treasures, and moments you bring to the table. Morelord Compendium gathers shared D&D 5e content into Foundry VTT packs, giving you a common place to browse the material available to your world.

  • Browse by the content you need. Organized packs include Actors, Items, Journals, Scenes, and Rollable Tables.
  • Keep useful session resources together. The package also includes Macro and Playlist packs.
  • Work inside Foundry. Use familiar compendium browsing to find the available entries.

For groups using this shared content collection, it is a practical companion to campaign preparation and play. Check the contents of the installed package for the specific entries available.

The current manifest declares Foundry VTT 13 as its minimum and verified version and identifies D&D 5e as its system. Foundry v14 support is not declared.
