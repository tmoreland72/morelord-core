Make the road as memorable as the destination.

A dangerous route. Dwindling supplies. A discovery that changes the plan. Morelord Journeys gives overland travel a place in your campaign's story, with a persistent Foundry VTT workflow that carries the expedition from departure through the day's challenges and into camp.

  • Give preparation a purpose. Plan the route, select travelers, assign expedition roles, and review the supplies the party actually carries.
  • Put choices into every travel day. Work through pace, weather, navigation, encounters, discoveries, and foraging with clear prompts and recorded results.
  • Keep players involved. Request the relevant character rolls while leaving consequential decisions with the table.
  • Carry the adventure into camp. Manage watches, shelter, supplies, and sleep checks instead of ending the journey at sunset.
  • Remember the road behind you. Preserve progress and expedition history across sessions.

Optional Morelord Encounters and Craftworks integrations connect travel to monster rosters and gathering workflows. Use the combination that fits your campaign, with the GM guiding the consequences.

Requires Foundry VTT v14, D&D 5e 5.3 or later, and Morelord Core 0.3.0 or later.
