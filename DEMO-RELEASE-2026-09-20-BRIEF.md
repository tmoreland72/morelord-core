## Proposed spoken script

### Opening

This is Chuck from Morelord Gaming. Today we're looking at the September twentieth updates, with new ways to prepare encounters, manage a journey, and turn suspicious monster bits into useful research. I'm logged in as the game master. Let's take a look.

### Encounters

Morelord Encounters now gives us three starting points: Combat Encounters, Guide Me Encounters, and Encounter Stories.

Guide Me has forty original situations for those moments when the party takes a left turn into your completely unprepared imagination. Choose the kind of interaction and pressure you want, then save the scene for later.

For something more developed, the Premium Encounter Stories workflow keeps editable GM journals and prepared combat rosters together. Here's Brother's Keeper, with player-safe read-aloud text and two hill giants. The giants require the Monster Manual content. Apparently one giant wasn't enough family drama.

Combat generation also keeps encounter strength more consistent, and Verify Party can include NPC companions from the primary party.

### Journeys

In Morelord Journeys, the dashboard shows how far we've traveled and how much remains. I can adjust the remaining journey before starting another day.

Continue now applies supplies as we advance, stopping for required shortage saves. And if I need to correct a step, Go Back can reverse Journey-applied changes. It checks for conflicting outside edits before restoring them.

Night encounters show the affected watch and time since camp began. Rest results explain interruptions and recovery, including four-hour Trance. Fewer mysteries about who actually got any sleep.

### Craftworks and Downtime

Morelord Craftworks makes marked recipes easier to follow during harvesting, with owned and required component counts. Long recipe names keep their quantities visible.

With Premium Morelord Downtime and accessible Drakkenheim content enabled in Craftworks, I can create a one-hour Research Drakkenheim Recipes project using a monster component from personal or party inventory.

Research doesn't consume the component. It can discover up to five matching recipes, and those discoveries become known to every player character. Clicking a result opens the Recipes browser. Project and Session histories also explain what happened in readable language.

### Marketplace and Compendium

Morelord Marketplace adds a temporary one-times buy and sell rate toggle for the global marketplace. Existing shop prices stay intact. Catalogs and restocks also respect excluded SRD sources, including copied items.

The personal Morelord Compendium update includes prepared shop portraits and tokens. Here's a quick look at those shopkeepers.

### Core and closing

Morelord Core ties these updates together with shared controls and improved troubleshooting exports. The next Core update adds a one-time reporting notice for new and existing worlds. Feature usage is selected by default, unless you've previously turned it off. Uncheck it before saving to opt out, or change it later in Core Settings. Error reporting is a separate choice, off by default. No account connection is needed.

There's also a Join Discord button in Core's settings for support and ideas.

Update Core alongside the gameplay modules. These releases were checked on Foundry fourteen, build three sixty-eight. You'll find the release notes and updated guides on the Morelord Gaming website. Better tools. Smoother sessions. More adventure.
