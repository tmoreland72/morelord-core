## Proposed narration

I'm logged in as the game master. We also have Darro, Snovlumi, Thalin, and Ubeeren logged in, each in a separate session. That lets us see the requests arrive just as they would for the players at the table.

We're ready to leave Neverwinter for Phandalin using Morelord Journeys. We can skip individual journey steps, but for this demonstration we'll use all of them.

We'll set Neverwinter as our origin and Phandalin as our destination. Based on our planned route and a normal travel pace, we're allowing five and a third days. Journeys measures travel in thirds of a day.

Route ratings can change each day. Today we're staying on the High Road, so we'll choose Safe or civilized for danger. Resources are Traveled or heavily settled land: plenty of travelers means less food left to forage. Discovery is Unlikely, and navigation is Simple. It's a road. Ideally, we keep it underneath us.

All four characters join the expedition. Thalin has our best Survival, so she'll navigate. She also has the best Perception, but she can only fill one role. Snovlumi takes the Observer role as our next-best option.

Before we create the journey, let's check the Supply Manifest together. We need food, water, and sleeping equipment, including anything held in the party inventory. Ubeeren can draw on the shared rations. And remember to fill those waterskins: an empty container is just something else to carry. We'll confirm the quantities cover our plan and discuss any extra purchases before leaving.

Now we can create the journey. Journeys saves our progress, so we can close this window and return whenever we're ready for the next step.

Here we are on day one. We'll confirm today's route ratings and supplies, then begin the travel day.

First is weather. We'll roll for extreme weather, then generate the forecast. An ordinary overcast day won't disrupt our travel plan, but a massive typhoon probably would.

Next is pace. We can travel slowly, normally, quickly, or stay put to rest. There's not a dragon chasing us today, so we'll choose Normal pace.

Now we request the day encounter rolls. Each party member receives a request in their own session and rolls the configured die. The game master sees the results. Any encounter can be treated as combat or non-combat; we'll save combat and the Morelord Encounters demonstration for day two.

Next is discovery. Snovlumi receives a Perception check as our Observer. With discovery set to Unlikely, finding something unusual on this well-traveled road is a tough check.  I get distracted easily so I would definitely followup on any discovery.  SQUIRREL!!

If a discovery appears, the game master can roll for an idea, and the party can investigate or keep moving. If we investigate, we'll record the time spent when we return to Journeys. A roadside curiosity might be a short detour. A newly discovered dungeon could keep us busy for days.

Navigation goes to Thalin. Our Simple route has a DC of five. A successful check keeps us on course; getting lost can delay our progress.

We can also choose to Press On for another third of a day. That sends Constitution saving throws to the party, and failures can add exhaustion automatically. Today we'll stick with our normal day's travel.

Now everyone gets a foraging check. Resources are scarce along the High Road, so this is a difficult roll. Once the results arrive, we'll review what the party found, check the proposed food and water allocations, and confirm which supplies are consumed.

We can also gather crafting materials through Morelord Craftworks. We'll launch Craftworks, choose Gather, and select Grassland. Each player can choose an available skill and make their gathering roll. Any materials found go to the character or party inventory according to the gathering configuration. Gathering materials is separate from resolving our food and water.

We'll close Craftworks and return to Journeys. It's time to make camp.

We'll assign our four two-hour watches, one to each traveler. Camp actions include watching, crafting, cooking, preparing, sleeping, or another task. Some actions need a campfire, so we'll light one and give Darro the Prepare action. We'll review its watch penalty and next-day benefit, while making sure everyone has enough time to sleep.

With our assignments ready, the game master rolls the night's encounter check. The result tells us whether the night is quiet or brings an encounter. Any interruption needs to be recorded before resolving rest.

Next we find out if we sleep like a baby or have constant nightmares of skeletons and zombies trying to eat us. Journeys checks each character's required sleep or meditation, their camp assignments, bedding, and any interruptions. Extra rest may be needed if the night was interrupted.

If a Peaceful Rest reward is available, we'll choose Heroic Inspiration. Missing a rest could also trigger a separate sleep-deprivation save.

Finally, we'll review the Expedition Log and complete the travel day. With one full day of progress, we're on day two with four and a third days remaining.

And there's one more thing to check in Morelord Downtime: our Sentinel Shield commission has advanced to one of its ten labor days. Brynja keeps working while we're on the road. Still no roadside delivery, though.
